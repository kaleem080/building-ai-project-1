# AI Customer Support & Inventory Assistant

Building AI course project

## Summary
This project aims to automate customer inquiries and basic inventory tracking for small retail businesses using Natural Language Processing and simple classification models. It helps streamline customer communication, answer common questions instantly, and track popular product demands.

## Background
Managing customer support and keeping track of product demand manually in local retail stores is time-consuming. Small business owners often spend hours replying to repetitive product queries instead of focusing on business growth. This AI tool solves this by providing automated, accurate responses and summarizing customer requests.

## How is it used?
Customers send messages or inquiries through a web form or chat interface. The system classifies the query into categories (e.g., pricing, availability, store hours) and generates appropriate responses or alerts the owner when manual assistance is needed.

## Data sources and AI methods
- Training data: A dataset of common customer inquiries, frequently asked questions, and product inventory details.
- AI Techniques: 
  - Text classification using TF-IDF and Naive Bayes / Logistic Regression.
  - Basic rule-based routing for critical inquiries.

## Challenges
- Accurately handling ambiguous customer messages or regional phrasing.
- Ensuring inventory data is updated in real-time.

## What next?
In the future, the project could integrate speech recognition for voice-based interactions and expand into automated supplier restocking alerts.
